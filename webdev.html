Why a web developer?

The main appeal of web development for me is....

the creativity aspect, i.e. indie web and aspiring to add life to web sites, make them an interactive and memorable experience rather than just another corporate website + infinite ways to execute the same brief.

the workflow of seeing something come together before your eyes and the satisfcation of seeing results straight away (particularly for front end work)

good opportunity to utilise what i know and build on it. there are new tools and frameworks to learn

widespread use of web developers i.e. good for a first position offering a pathway to decide how to specialise, both technically but also what sector + can work in many different sectors and with many different clients. i think the opportunity for diversity in who you are working with and what you are making will always push you to be better at what you are doing and understand its fundamentals better
