Why a web developer?

One of the main appeals of web development for me is the creativity aspect. Making a webpage is such a satisfying way to see an idea take shape and come to life. I am continuously inspired by the indie web when witnessing the endlessly creative forms that websites can take. I aspire to also be able to create interactive and memorable experiences in the future.

Additionally, I am interested to move into a career as a web developer as I enjoy the workflow of building sites. I like to plan out how to approach each brief and piece it together as a puzzle - it is quite rewarding work. I also believe that it will be a good opportunity for me to utilise what I already know and build on it. There are always tools and frameworks to learn and I love to be learning continually.

Finally, web development is also a particularly interesting career route based on how widespread it is and also due to it's ongoing evolution. I think this makes it offer a unique opportunity to explore a range of work sectors as well as specialise in different technical domains. This potential for diversity in who you are working with and what you are buidling is appealing to me. Besides, I believe that working in an ever-changing and evolving environment will always push you to better your skills and enhance your knowledge.
